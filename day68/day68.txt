Day 68 Notes

Commands Learned:
- ls -l
- whoami
- id

Important Concepts:
- File Owner
- Group Owner
- Ownership

Example:
-rw-r--r--  1 jobin  staff  0 Jun 17 ownership.txt

Owner = jobin
Group = staff

Most Important Learning:
Every file in Linux has an owner and a group. Ownership controls who can access and modify files.


Ownership Verification:

fileA.txt -> Owner: jobin, Group: staff
fileB.txt -> Owner: jobin, Group: staff

Observation:
New files are automatically owned by the user who creates them.
