Day 63 Notes

Commands Learned:
- ls -l
- chmod
- whoami
- id

Important Concepts:
- File Owner
- Group Owner
- Read Permission (r)
- Write Permission (w)
- Execute Permission (x)

Practical Examples:
- chmod 755 file1.txt
- chmod 644 file1.txt

Most Important Learning:
Linux permissions determine who can read, write, and execute files.
